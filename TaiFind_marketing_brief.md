# Marketing brief — TaiFind

Use this document to align landing pages, App Store copy, social posts, and partner outreach. Adjust tone per channel (see Voice).

---

## Product snapshot

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Name** | TaiFind |
| **Platform** | iPhone (iOS app) |
| **Category (store)** | Lifestyle (see Xcode `LSApplicationCategoryType`) |
| **One-line pitch** | See Taiwan’s official air quality on a map—plus YouBike and Taipei trash cans when you need them. |
| **Problem** | Built-in phone weather often doesn’t give people in Taiwan the same clarity on AQI and local stations they want for daily decisions. |
| **Solution** | A map-first app using government-aligned readings and open data, with optional urban utility layers. |

---

## Audience

| Segment | Why they care |
|---------|----------------|
| **Residents (Taipei / Taichung / island-wide)** | Daily planning: exercise outdoors, kids, masks, window ventilation. |
| **Visitors & students** | Unfamiliar with local AQI conventions; want one trustworthy map. |
| **Cyclists & commuters** | YouBike availability next to air context for the same trip. |
| **Eco- / health-conscious users** | Pollutant-level curiosity beyond a single index number. |

**Non-goals for messaging:** Do not claim medical advice; position as an informational map built on official and open data.

---

## Positioning

**Primary:** *Taiwan AQI on a map—official tiers, your neighborhood.*

**Secondary:** *Optional layers: YouBike Taipei & Taichung, Taipei public trash cans.*

**Differentiator:** Map + Taiwan MoE-style color logic + pollutant detail sheets + bilingual UI—not a generic global AQI widget.

**Competitive angle (respectful):** Many users rely on the stock Weather app; TaiFind is **purpose-built for Taiwan’s stations and standards**, with extra local utility layers.

---

## Key messages (copy blocks)

### Short (≤280 characters, social / SMS)

1. **TaiFind puts Taiwan’s official air quality on a map—tap a station for AQI, PM2.5, ozone, and more. English & 繁體中文.**
2. **Planning a ride? Switch layers to see YouBike bikes and docks in Taipei or Taichung without leaving the map.**
3. **Finding a public trash can in Taipei shouldn’t be a treasure hunt. Toggle the layer and walk to the nearest bin.**

### Medium (hero subhead / App Store subtitle direction)

- **Map Taiwan’s air quality with ministry-aligned colors and numbers. Add YouBike or Taipei trash cans when you need them—all in one place.**

### Long (landing “story” paragraph)

TaiFind helps you see what you’re breathing—station by station—using readings presented in line with Taiwan’s air quality framework. Zoom the heatmap on bad days, tap through to pollutant details, or switch to YouBike layers to pair transit choices with the air picture. Built with open data and designed for both English and Traditional Chinese readers.

---

## Feature bullets (pick 3–5 for any given asset)

- Official-style **AQI** and **pollutant** map (SO₂, CO, O₃, PM₁₀, PM₂.₅, NO₂)
- **Pin** or **heatmap** visualization + color legend tied to Taiwan tiers
- **Station detail** sheets: status, main pollutant, last update
- **Map styles:** standard, satellite, hybrid
- **YouBike 2.0** for **Taipei** and **Taichung** (available bikes & empty docks)
- **Public trash cans** layer for **Taipei** (clustered pins)
- **Location** button to jump to where you are
- **Bilingual** UI (EN / 繁體中文)

---

## Proof & trust

- Data attribution: Taiwan government open data and Ministry of Environment–style indicators (detail in-app).
- Transparent **Information** screen: methodology pointers, links to official sites, disclaimer.
- Developer presence: **bzbee.co**, built in Taipei (matches in-app footer).

Use trust language: *“based on open / official sources”* — avoid *“real-time guaranteed”* unless you add verified SLAs.

---

## Voice & tone

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Clear, calm, practical | Alarmist (“toxic sky tomorrow”) |
| Bilingual-friendly short sentences | Untranslated jargon without context |
| Credit sources | Imply Apple or government endorsement |

**English:** Friendly expert neighbor. **Chinese:** 清楚、實用、不製造恐慌。

## Landing page outline (suggested sections)

1. **Hero** — Name, one-line pitch, App Store button, device mockup (map + legend).
2. **Why TaiFind** — Problem (weather app gap) + solution (Taiwan-focused map).
3. **Features** — Three columns: Air / YouBike / Trash cans.

5. **Data & privacy** — Open data, location only when in use, link to privacy policy if published.
6. **FAQ** — e.g., “Is this medical advice?”, “Which cities for YouBike?”, “What about other counties?”
7. **Footer** — bzbee.co, contact email from app, social links.
